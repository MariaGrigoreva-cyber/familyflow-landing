VISUAL IDENTITY: «Семейный поток»

Create a consistent editorial visual for a modern family finance product.

The image should feel like part of one recognizable visual system,
not like generic AI stock photography.

====================
MOOD
====================

- calm
- warm
- contemporary
- intelligent
- trustworthy
- optimistic
- financially responsible without looking restrictive
- everyday real life
- subtle premium feeling

Money should feel manageable rather than stressful.

====================
VISUAL DIRECTION
====================

Modern editorial lifestyle illustration / editorial photography aesthetic.

Clean composition.

Soft natural light.

Muted natural palette.

Warm neutrals with subtle fresh green accents.

Comfortable contemporary home environments.

Enough negative space.

Natural everyday objects.

Images should look suitable for a modern fintech or family lifestyle publication.

====================
PEOPLE
====================

When people are shown:

- adults approximately 25–45 years old;
- natural appearance;
- ordinary modern families and couples;
- believable emotions;
- modern casual clothing;
- believable everyday situations;
- people should not look like corporate stock models.

Show a variety of family situations when appropriate.

Avoid exaggerated happiness, panic or theatrical expressions.

====================
POSSIBLE SCENES
====================

Depending on the article topic:

- couple discussing monthly plans at home;
- person calmly planning upcoming expenses;
- grocery shopping;
- online shopping decision;
- family breakfast and planning;
- planning a vacation;
- bills and ordinary household expenses;
- laptop or smartphone being used for planning;
- calendar and everyday objects;
- salary planning;
- saving for a larger goal;
- deciding whether a purchase fits into the month's plan.

====================
IMPORTANT
====================

Do NOT render readable text.

Do NOT add:
- captions;
- interface labels;
- fake app screens with readable text;
- logos;
- brand names;
- numbers;
- currency amounts.

If a smartphone or laptop appears,
its screen should be abstract, subtle and non-readable.

====================
AVOID
====================

Avoid clichés:

- piggy banks;
- raining money;
- piles of banknotes;
- flying coins;
- giant dollar signs;
- crypto imagery;
- calculators surrounded by cash;
- businessmen in suits;
- Wall Street imagery;
- luxury cars;
- mansions;
- champagne;
- exaggerated poverty;
- stressed person holding their head;
- generic corporate stock photography.

Avoid:
- surreal anatomy;
- excessive fingers;
- strange hands;
- plastic faces;
- overly glossy AI aesthetics;
- strong HDR;
- excessive saturation;
- cluttered compositions.

====================
FINAL FEELING
====================

The viewer should think:

«Это про обычную жизнь и деньги, которые хочется наконец-то спокойно держать под контролем».

The visuals should support the brand idea:
money planning can feel calm, clear and manageable.
